Risk assessment before submission

Answer the questions below to assess the risk related to using AI in your assignment. The result shows whether your submission appears to have low, medium, or high risk.

Choose the answer that fits best. Critical questions, such as rules, fact-checking, academic integrity, and privacy, carry more weight in the overall assessment.

Rules and guidelines

Have you checked whether AI is allowed in this assignment and followed the applicable rules?

Documentation of AI use

Do you have an overview of which AI tools you used and how you used them?

Fact-checking

Have you verified facts, quotes, and claims from AI using reliable sources?

Own work

Does your assignment include your own thoughts, evaluations, and reflections?

Referencing AI

Have you disclosed AI usage if required by your teacher or institution?

Learning objectives

Has AI supported your learning instead of doing the work for you?

Academic integrity

Are you confident that your submission is honest and does not misrepresent who did the work?

Privacy

Have you avoided sharing sensitive or personal information with AI tools?
